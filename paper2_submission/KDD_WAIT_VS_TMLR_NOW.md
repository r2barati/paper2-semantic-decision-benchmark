# KDD wait versus TMLR now

## Decision: SUBMIT TMLR NOW

As of 2026-08-26, the official KDD 2027 first-cycle deadline listed for the
Research and Datasets & Benchmarks tracks was July 26, 2026. The official KDD
FAQ says the February cycle date is still to be confirmed and that the February
cycle primarily permits the immediately preceding KDD resubmit decisions; a
rejected August-cycle paper is not simply eligible as a fresh February
resubmission.

Paper 2’s strongest identity is a rigorous empirical-methodology study, not a
new KDD-scale dataset. KDD Datasets & Benchmarks is therefore a plausible
fallback, while KDD Research would require a stronger data-science novelty
signal and an 8-page ACM format. Waiting would delay a rolling TMLR submission
without a published February date or a guaranteed fit advantage.

Reconsider KDD only if a future official call provides a date and the authors
choose a materially different KDD-specific paper identity without dual
submission overlap.

Sources: [KDD 2027 Research CFP](https://kdd2027.kdd.org/research-track-call-for-papers/),
[KDD Datasets & Benchmarks CFP](https://kdd2027.kdd.org/datasets-and-benchmarks-track-call-for-papers/),
and [KDD FAQ](https://kdd2027.kdd.org/frequently-asked-questions/).
