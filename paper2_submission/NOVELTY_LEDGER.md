# Novelty ledger

| Layer | Defensible contribution | Must not claim |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific formulation | interpretation → belief → controller → sequential utility is made measurable in one executable contract | a new universal principle that prediction differs from decision value |
| Empirical finding | accuracy, calibration, controller choice, and dynamics can produce different value rankings | causal universality beyond the tested design |
| Methodology | OIV/SIVR-style diagnostics, paired evaluation, hierarchical uncertainty, and explicit oracle/reference roles | SIVR as a universal or bounded information metric |
| Replication | held-out Phase 8B operational-complexity transfer and Phase 9 supply-side transfer | arbitrary event/topology or real-world generalization |
| Benchmark | reproducible semantic interpreters with fixed downstream control and cached LLM replay | a new inventory simulator or LLM benchmark as the main novelty |
| Infrastructure | frozen templates, seeds, manifests, tests, runners, and raw/corrected tables | production readiness |

## Positioning

Paper 2 is primarily empirical science plus evaluation methodology, with
benchmark infrastructure as a supporting contribution. The central unit is
semantic sensing under a fixed controller; resource selection/orchestration is
Paper 3 and must remain out of the contribution list.
