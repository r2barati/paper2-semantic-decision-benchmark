# TMLR OpenReview author checklist

Status: **HUMAN_ACTION_REQUIRED**. No author metadata is inferred.

Before submission, the authors must confirm:

- final author list, order, affiliations, and complete OpenReview profiles;
- publication history and conflict-of-interest declarations for every author;
- recommended Action Editors and any prohibited/conflicted editors;
- funding, competing interests, ethics, and human-subjects/IRB applicability;
- whether any concurrent archival submission or accepted publication creates
  overlap;
- whether the submission quota is available;
- final anonymous PDF and supplement contain no identifying links, names,
  acknowledgments, paths, or metadata;
- all authors reviewed the manuscript and take responsibility for it;
- any required TMLR AI-use disclosure is accurate and author-confirmed.

The policy sources are the current [TMLR Author Guide](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html),
[Editorial Policies](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html), and
[Submission Instructions](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/submissions.html).
