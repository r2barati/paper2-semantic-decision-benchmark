# TMLR positioning audit

Audit date: 2026-08-26. Official sources: [Author Guide](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html), [Editorial Policies](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html), [Acceptance Criteria](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html), and [Submission Instructions](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/submissions.html).

## Policy-grounded fit

TMLR asks whether the claims are supported by accurate, convincing, clear evidence and whether at least some TMLR readers would be interested in the findings. Method novelty is not itself necessary for acceptance. That does not make novelty irrelevant: originality, correctness, reader interest, accurate novelty claims, and non-overlap still matter.

The paper fits the explicit scope categories for experimental studies yielding new insight into learning systems, applications revealing strengths and weaknesses of methods, and performance-assessment frameworks/tasks. Its claim is an empirical characterization of the semantic-belief-to-sequential-utility interface, not a universal algorithmic or operational claim.

## Positioning decision

Primary identity: empirical ML science.

Secondary identity: consequential evaluation methodology.

The benchmark is supporting infrastructure. The paper should lead with the accuracy/calibration/value dissociation, controller mediation, held-out transfer, and boundary diagnostics. It should explicitly acknowledge decision-focused learning, value-of-information theory, decision calibration, and operational benchmark predecessors.

## Submission requirements still requiring people

Double-blind PDF and supplement, complete OpenReview profiles, conflicts, recommended Action Editors, funding/competing-interest declarations, human-subjects applicability, AI-use disclosure, and final originality review remain human author responsibilities.
