# Post-correction paper-readiness audit

## VERDICT B — Paper ready with disclosures

The material estimand, metric, bootstrap, multiplicity, terminology, and
reproducibility problems are corrected without new simulations. The paper is
ready only with the narrower claim hierarchy and disclosures below.

1. **Primary estimand.** Balanced benchmark performance over the deliberately
   constructed evaluation set: equal regime weight, then equal template-family,
   variant, and paired-seed weight.
2. **Deployment-prior estimand.** Expected value under the prior actually
   declared or passed to NoInfo. Phase 8B is 70/30 Normal/DemandSurge; Phase
   6/7 use the code-executed 0.35/0.35/0.30 prior; Phase 9 uses the
   code-executed 0.50/0.50 prior. It is always reported separately.
3. **Headline SIVR changes.** Positive-OIV Phase 8B/9A values are unchanged
   by replacing an absolute denominator with the signed denominator. In Phase
   9B `long_lead` and `lost_sales`, negative OIV cases now carry status
   `NEGATIVE_ORACLE_REFERENCE_VALUE`; signed values reverse relative to the
   historical absolute-denominator diagnostic when the sensor beats NoInfo.
   Near-zero OIV returns NaN with
   `ZERO_OR_NEAR_ZERO_REFERENCE_VALUE`.
4. **Raw reward effects.** Phase 8B balanced deltas versus NoInfo remain
   positive: RuleBased +101.0, raw TF-IDF +48.5, calibrated TF-IDF +70.8,
   and gpt-4o +67.1 reward units. These, with regime-specific deltas and CIs,
   are primary; SIVR is secondary.
5. **Phase-7 calibration.** Remains supported: raw TF-IDF accuracy 0.889 vs
   calibrated 0.861, while standard multiclass Brier is 0.552 vs 0.240 and
   log-loss is 0.932 vs 0.397.
6. **Phase-8B generalization.** Remains supported as held-out linguistic
   generalization plus operational-complexity replication under the fixed
   BeliefAdaptiveController. It does not establish controller-independent
   value.
7. **Phase-9A.** Establishes cross-event operational transfer to a
   supplier-capacity-drop environment. The original all-template analysis is
   not clean linguistic generalization because it includes TF-IDF training
   templates; the saved test-template-only analysis is reported separately.
8. **Phase-9B.** Establishes descriptive robustness/boundary behavior of the
   fixed controller, not broad generalization. `long_lead` and `lost_sales`
   have OracleSemantic rewards below NoInfo (OIV -90.94 and -72.34,
   respectively). Their primary classification is controller/environment
   mismatch with an absent positive oracle reference, not automatically
   semantic sensing failure.
9. **Oracle underperformance.** Yes. In Phase 8B Normal, OracleSemantic is
   439.6 versus NoInfo 485.7. In Phase 9A Normal, OracleSemantic is 1166.7
   versus NoInfo 1333.6 in the saved regime-level results. OracleSemantic is
   perfect semantic belief through the same fixed controller, not an optimal
   policy or reward upper bound.
10. **SIVR consistency.** All publication recomputations call exactly one
    implementation, `src.metrics.signed_sivr`, with a signed denominator and
    explicit statuses. No absolute denominator is used in corrected
    publication analysis.
11. **Bootstrap.** Publication CIs use regime-stratified family resampling,
    variant resampling within family, and paired seed resampling within
    variant. Balanced and prior-weighted estimands are separate, and the RNG
    seed is recorded.
12. **Multiplicity.** Phase 8B and 9A sensor comparisons use Holm-adjusted
    p-values. Phase 9B is explicitly descriptive robustness/boundary analysis;
    OFAT design is not treated as eliminating multiplicity.
13. **Brier score.** Corrected quality tables use conventional multiclass
    `sum_k (p_k-y_k)^2`, not the true-class-only squared error.
14. **Clean clone.** The final candidate commit
    the final candidate commit recorded by Git clones cleanly, resolves the
    pinned environment specification, loads the vendored Paper-1 Gym at
    `a745fd5`, regenerates corrected tables and the ledger, and passes 273
    tests with 0 failures.
15. **GPT offline replay.** Published semantic outputs are tracked under
    `results/frozen_llm_outputs/`, the historical `.llm_cache` is also tracked,
    and the runner replays frozen results without an API key.
16. **Scientific ledger.** `SCIENTIFIC_LEDGER.md` is regenerated by
    `results/publication/generate_scientific_ledger.py` from corrected audit
    CSVs. The pre-correction ledger is preserved in the audit snapshot.
17. **New experiments.** None are required to resolve the corrected validity
    questions. Additional experiments would be optional future work, not a
    response to less favorable corrected numbers.

## Claim hierarchy

Supported: semantic beliefs can materially alter operational reward;
calibration can change realized decision value; accuracy alone does not rank
utility reliably; classical NLP can rival or outperform GPT in this benchmark;
held-out Phase 8B effects survive into a richer multi-echelon environment; and
controller/environment mismatch creates boundary conditions.

Not established: LLM superiority, universal semantic information value,
controller-independent value, industrial deployment effectiveness, arbitrary
event or topology generalization, or universal SIVR robustness.

The exact machine-readable evidence is in `sivr_recomputed.csv`,
`raw_reward_effects.csv`, `phase9b_boundary_analysis.csv`, and the generated
publication tables. Historical Phase 7/8A/8B/9 outputs are retained under
`results/correction_audit/pre_correction_snapshot/` and their original result
directories.
